How it works

Most teams test 5-10 video ads per month. That's not enough data to find patterns. You might get lucky, but you can't repeat it.

We produce 40-70 videos per batch. Real creators on camera, AI-generated visuals, product demos. Each video tests a specific idea: a pain point, a hook, a format, a person.

We write scripts in modules. One proven body, multiple openings. One concept, multiple creators. When something converts, we know exactly what worked and why -- so we can make more of it.

After 2-3 weeks of running as ads, we have real purchase data. Not likes, not views -- actual customers. We tag every creative as Scale, Iterate, or Kill. Then we scale the winners and test new ideas.

What's inside each batch

Everything you need to go from zero to tested at scale.

Angles

5-8 reasons people buy your product

We break your product into real pain points. For supplements: bloating, fatigue, skin, sleep. For AI products: speed, social proof, before/after. Each angle becomes a testing track.

Scripts

Modular: hook + body + CTA

At least 3 different openings per concept. Written from research -- your competitors' ads, customer surveys, your own ad data. Not guesswork.

Creators

Same script, different people, different results

2-3 creators per batch from our 600+ network. We test the same script with different people. The same script can cost $32 per customer with one person and $92 with another.

Formats

6 formats, tested -- not assumed

UGC talking head, voiceover + B-roll, before/after, product demo, POV. In our data, voiceover + B-roll cost 25% less per customer than talking heads.

AI Visuals

Static ads and AI-generated scenes at half the cost

AI statics brought customers at half the cost of traditional statics in our tests. We use AI where it performs, not where it's trendy.

What we learned testing 150+ creatives

Patterns from real ad data. Not theory -- things that changed how we spend our clients' money.

The person matters more than the script

Same hook. Same script. Same product. Creator A brought customers at $32 each. Creator B: $92. The difference isn't talent -- it's fit between the person and the message.

3x cost difference, same creative

Voiceover + B-roll beats talking head

We spent 65% of budget on creators talking to camera. It cost $70 per customer. Voiceover with product footage got only 33% of budget but brought customers at $53 each.

$53 vs $70 per customer

Under 20 seconds wins

Shorter videos cost 1.5x less per customer. Not because of attention spans -- because shorter videos force you to write sharper openings.

1.5x cheaper per customer

All cost differences come from the creative

The cost to show an ad was stable at $30 across all our videos. Every difference in customer cost came from the creative itself -- not the algorithm, not the bidding.

Three batches, two months

By the end of month 2, you have 100-150 tested creatives, a clear picture of what works, and a system to keep finding new winners.

Batch 1

Find what works

40-70 original videos. Multiple angles, creators, formats. Every video is a hypothesis with a clear pass/fail metric. After 2-3 weeks of ad data, we know which combinations sell.

Batch 2

Scale the winners

10+ variations of every winner from Batch 1. New openings, re-edited middle sections, different calls to action. Budget follows performance, not volume.

Batch 3

Find more

50 new concepts testing untested ideas -- different pain points, new audiences, formats we haven't tried yet. Runs in parallel with Batch 2.
