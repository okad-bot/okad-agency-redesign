How it works

Most teams test 5-10 video ads per month. That's not enough data to find patterns. You might get lucky, but you can't repeat it.

We produce 40-70 videos per batch. Real creators on camera, AI-generated visuals, product demos. Each video tests a specific idea: a pain point, a hook, a format, a person.

We write scripts in modules. One proven body, multiple openings. One concept, multiple creators. When something converts, we know exactly what worked and why -- so we can make more of it.

After 2-3 weeks of running as ads, we have real purchase data. Not likes, not views -- actual customers. We tag every creative as Scale, Iterate, or Kill. Then we scale the winners and test new ideas.

What's inside each batch

Angles
We break your product into 5-8 reasons people actually buy. For supplements, that's bloating, fatigue, skin, sleep. For an AI product, it's speed, social proof, before/after. Each angle becomes a testing track.
Scripts
Modular: hook + body + call to action. At least 3 different openings per concept. Written from research -- your competitors' ads, customer surveys, your own ad account data. Not guesswork.
Creators
2-3 creators per batch from our 600+ network. We test the same script with different people -- not to see who looks better, but to find who fits which message. The same script can cost $32 per customer with one person and $92 with another.
Formats
UGC talking head, voiceover + B-roll, before/after, product demo, POV. We test which format works for your audience instead of assuming. In our data, voiceover + B-roll cost 25% less per customer than talking heads.
AI visuals
Static ads, AI-generated scenes, product mockups. In our tests, AI statics brought customers at half the cost of traditional statics. We use AI where it performs, not where it's trendy.

What we learned testing 150+ creatives

These are patterns we found from real ad data. Not theory -- things that changed how we spend our clients' money.

The person matters more than the script

Same hook. Same script. Same product. Creator A brought customers at $32 each. Creator B: $92. The difference isn't talent -- it's fit between the person and the message they're delivering.

3x cost difference, same creative

Voiceover + B-roll beats talking head

We spent 65% of budget on creators talking to camera. It cost $70 per customer. Voiceover with product footage got only 33% of budget but brought customers at $53 each. We reallocated.

$53 vs $70 per customer

Under 20 seconds wins

Shorter videos cost 1.5x less per customer. Not because of attention spans -- because shorter videos force you to write sharper openings. The constraint makes the creative better.

1.5x cheaper per customer

All cost differences come from the creative

The cost to show an ad (CPM) was stable at $30 across all our videos. Every difference in customer cost came from the creative itself -- not the algorithm, not the audience, not the bidding.

$30 median CPM across all creatives

Three batches, two months

By the end of month 2, you have 100-150 tested creatives, a clear picture of what works, and a system to keep finding new winners.

Batch 1

Find what works

40-70 original videos. Multiple angles, creators, formats. Every video is a hypothesis with a clear pass/fail metric. After 2-3 weeks of ad data, we know which combinations sell.

Batch 2

Scale the winners

10+ variations of every winner from Batch 1. New openings, re-edited middle sections, different calls to action. Budget follows performance, not volume.

Batch 3

Find more

50 new concepts testing untested ideas -- different pain points, new audiences, formats we haven't tried yet. Runs in parallel with Batch 2, so you never stop finding winners.
